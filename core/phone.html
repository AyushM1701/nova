“Hey Siri, Ask Nova”

Make a Shortcut once, then talk to Nova through Siri, the Action Button, your Apple Watch or CarPlay. Siri reads Nova's answer out loud; the full reply shows up here too.

  1. Open the Shortcuts app, tap +, and name the shortcut Ask Nova.
  2. Add Dictate Text.
  3. Add URL Encode. It encodes the Dictated Text.
  4. Add Get Contents of URL. Tap its URL, paste the Siri link, then put the cursor at the very end and pick URL Encoded Text from the bar above the keyboard.
  5. Add Speak Text. It speaks the Contents of URL. Tap Done.

Now say “Hey Siri, Ask Nova”, then your request. The first time, allow the shortcut to connect to your computer. For the Action Button: Settings → Action Button → Shortcut → Ask Nova.

The link holds a key that can only send requests: it can’t read your conversation or Allow anything. Still keep it to yourself. Forget phones in Nova’s Settings makes it stop working.
