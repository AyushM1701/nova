                  Nova listens on its Tailscale address and nowhere on Wi-Fi. Tailscale encrypts the traffic; plain Wi-Fi doesn't, so anyone on the same network could read the pairing key. Needs Tailscale on this computer and on your phone. Until you choose, Nova uses only Tailscale whenever the Tailscale app is installed here. Off, the phone also works on your home Wi-Fi.

                  Pair your phone

                  Pair with the Tailscale code if you can (Tailscale on, on the phone): its address never changes, it works at home and away, and it's encrypted. The Home Wi-Fi address can change when your router restarts, and then you'd have to scan again.
