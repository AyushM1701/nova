          Your browsers

          Nova can read pages, open tabs, click and type in the browsers you turn on here, inside your own profile with your logins. It asks before anything that submits, buys, posts or sends, and never types passwords, card numbers or one-time codes. Say “in Brave” to use another browser for one request.

          Routines

          One phrase, several steps. Say the phrase (or “switch to … mode”) and Nova does every step, asking first for anything risky. “Start my day” is built in: a short spoken rundown of your projects' branches, open tasks in your notes and follow-ups from memory.

          Watch a long command

          Run it through Nova and keep working; Nova tells you when it finishes, and if it fails, why, and offers to fix it.

          Share what you're looking at

          Tells the AI which app you're in, and when you ask about your screen (“what's this error?”) lets it see a screenshot. Off means the AI only gets your words.

          Remember what you copy

          The last 20 things you copied, kept in memory only and gone when Nova quits, so you can say “paste the link I copied earlier”. Anything that looks like a password or key is skipped. The AI only sees them when you mention copying or the clipboard.

          Answer permission requests by voice

          When Nova asks for your OK during a spoken request, say “allow” or “deny” (or “yes”, “go ahead”, “no”, “cancel”). Only a short, clear answer counts. Turn this off if other people talk near your computer and you'd rather always click or press a key.

          Time to answer a permission request

          When the AI wants to delete files, install software or push code, Nova asks first. No answer means no.
